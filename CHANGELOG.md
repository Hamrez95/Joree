## v0.3.0 — Workplace AI, one lesson at a time

- Eight Persian lessons, 83 substantial cards, 24 answer-reveal exercises, four local diagrams, and official references.
- A dependency-free practice lab with synthetic documents, access filters, mock tools, and optional local inference.

## v0.2.3 — First public preview

The little console is ready to leave its folder.

- Persian-first RTL flashcards with a pastel handheld-console shell.
- Course library, simple Markdown import, preview/error states, chapters, study, and question/answer reveal.
- Recall dates, bookmarks, notes, real local progress, and milestones.
- Adjustable text, reduced/disabled motion, optional button sound, and blue/lavender colors.
- Blocks, Snake, and Pong with pause and local scores.
- Windows .NET / WebView2 host, SQLite persistence, tray, compact mode, single-instance behavior, and saved window placement.
- Per-user installer, desktop/Start shortcuts, a new جرعه icon, and JSON backups.
- English setup/developer docs, Persian guides, lesson templates, actual captures, and separate promotional illustrations.

Still brewing: advanced lesson metadata/stable author IDs, optional sign-in startup, native reminders, and the garden theme. Preview binaries are unsigned and need shared .NET 10 Desktop and WebView2 runtimes.
