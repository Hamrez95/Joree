# Joree v0.3.5 — Fewer places to get stuck ☕

This update makes the collection shelf collapsible, keeps Settings inside the visible console, and gives lesson text more comfortable reading controls.

## What changed

- The collection list now opens and closes from its heading, and closes after selecting a collection.
- Settings no longer expands beyond the app window; its content scrolls while the back button stays visible.
- Added smaller lesson text sizes without changing existing size choices.
- Justified Persian lesson paragraphs for a cleaner reading edge.
- Moved the expand/compact control into the top window-control row, where it is easier to find and use.

## Downloads

- **JoreeSetup.exe** installs for your Windows account without administrator elevation.
- **Joree-0.3.5-win-x64.zip** is the extract-and-run build.
- The release includes a lesson template, installation notes, and SHA-256 checksums.

Requires 64-bit Windows, .NET 10 Desktop Runtime x64, and Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime.
